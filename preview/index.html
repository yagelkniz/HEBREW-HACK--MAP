      How to play SNAP
      1. 🔊 Listen to the word.
      2. 📸 Pick a camera. Who's in the sentence: me, them, or you?
      3. 👆 Tap the people. Pick the right number, and boys or girls.
      4. 📸 Press Snap!

      You need the right camera AND the right people.

      Pick your camera 📸
      🤳 Selfie = I'm in itani (I) · anakhnu (we)e.g. "anakhnu" → Selfie + friends
      📷 Camera = I talk ABOUT themhu (he) · hi (she) · hem (they) · hen (they, all girls)e.g. "hi" → Camera + 1 girl
      📱 Video call = I talk TO themata (you, boy) · at (you, girl) · atem (you all) · aten (you all, all girls)e.g. "at" → Video call + 1 girl

      👦 + 👧 = boys! If even one boy is in the group, use hem / atem.
